# M16: a simulated ceremony, carried through to a live-chain run

Run 2026-11-01 and 2026-11-02 in WSL2 Ubuntu 24.04, with Bitcoin Core v31.1.0 and snarkjs 0.7.5.

## The question

Every milestone so far proved with an insecure, deterministic Groth16 setup and ran on regtest,
where the scenario mines its own blocks. M16 does two things:

- replaces the setup with a multi-party phase 2 closed by a Bitcoin block hash;
- runs the anchored, bound graph (M9, M13) with that key on a chain whose blocks the scenario
  does not control.

## 1. The ceremony, simulated

[`ceremony_sim.sh`](ceremony_sim.sh) runs phase 2 of `accounting16x4_committed` (the circuit M1,
M2, M9 and M13 settle). Results are in [`results/`](results/).

1. **The beacon is named first.** Before any contribution, the transcript records that the beacon
   will be the hash of testnet4 block 158478. That block did not exist yet (tip 158477).
2. **Three contributors.** Each is its own Linux user (`zkc-contrib-1..3`, uids 1002–1004) with
   its own home directory. Each reads the previous key from a hand-off directory and contributes
   64 bytes of its own `/dev/urandom`, held in a shell variable and never written to disk. It
   writes the next key and its contribution hash, then deletes its copies. About 1.5 s each.
3. **The beacon.** Block 158478 arrived at 11:44 UTC with hash
   `00000000569882f18a806f83f272c1e438ad5f0207694e81cabbfa40619e8058`. `snarkjs zkey beacon`
   applied it with 2^10 iterations.
4. **Verification.** `snarkjs zkey verify` over the whole chain against the circuit and the
   phase-1 file: `ZKey Ok!`, listing the three contributions and the beacon with their hashes.
   Verifying key sha256 `e1574341…291f8ae1`.

| Step | Time | Peak memory |
|---|---|---|
| Setup and three contributions | 20.2 s | 605 MiB |
| Beacon, verify, export | 16.2 s | 631 MiB |

**What the simulation does not give.** The three contributors share one machine and one
administrator, so this is the procedure, not independence. Phase 1 is M6's local power-20 file,
which has one insecure beacon. A real run makes two changes:

- **Phase 1:** pass the public Hermez/Perpetual Powers of Tau file as the second argument.
  `powersOfTau28_hez_final_20.ptau`, about 1.2 GB, with its hash checked against snarkjs's list.
- **Phase 2:** each contributor runs the contribution step on their own machine.

## 2. BitVM on the ceremony's key

`zkc-m1 prepare` on the new verifying key took 163 s with a 13.0 GB peak.

- The tampered assertion is disprovable at leaf 431, as with M1's key.
- The two keys share gamma but differ in delta and in IC. 49 of the 432 disprove leaves change
  size, and the total moves by 6 bytes.
- The largest leaf is unchanged: 3,948,062 bytes, leaf 425. So M12's block-fit margin holds for
  this key too. M14 compared two keys from the same beacon and saw only 5 leaves change; a real
  ceremony moves more of them.

## 3. The driver on a live chain

`zkc-m2` gained, through the environment:

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| `ZKC_CHAIN=live` | Blocks come from the network. The scenario waits for heights instead of mining; transactions are sent and awaited; expected rejections are checked against the mempool only (no block is built to test them), and a probe the chain overtakes between polls is recorded as missed, not failed |
| `ZKC_PRESIGN_SAT_PER_KVB`, `ZKC_MARKET_SAT_PER_KVB`, `ZKC_MAX_SAT_PER_KVB` | Fee rates in sat/kvB throughout, so the graph can presign at testnet4's relay floor of 100 sat/kvB (0.1 sat/vB) |
| `ZKC_BOND_SATS`, `ZKC_BURN_SATS`, `ZKC_DEADLINE_SATS` | Amounts sized to testnet coins |
| `ZKC_KEY_SALT` | A secret mixed into every demo key and the Winternitz seed. On a public chain, keys from public labels alone are anyone's |
| live runs only | The wallet imports every key the run's outputs pay as a `tr(KEY)` descriptor, so bond returns, payouts and refunds come back to it and fund the next run |

[`batch.py --small`](../bitvm/batch.py) writes the testnet batch: M1's four sales and three buyers
at about 1/500 of the amounts, 10,000 sats of escrow, every output above the dust limit.
[`prove_batch.sh`](prove_batch.sh) proves it with the ceremony's key; snarkjs verifies it against
the ceremony's verifying key, and circomlibjs recomputes its sale commitment.

[`run-m16.sh`](../bitvm/scripts/run-m16.sh) runs the scenarios:

- everything at 100 sat/kvB, presigned or signed when posted; the wallet funds at 1 sat/vB;
- a 5,000-sat bond, a 2,500-sat burn and a 1,000-sat deadline connector;
- the challenge priced at the presigned rate.

testnet4's blocks take 100 sat/kvB transactions, so no fee spike is modelled here; M9 and M13
cover spikes.

**The rehearsal.** `run-m16.sh <work> rehearse` runs on a private regtest node that mines a block
every 15 s on a timer, whatever the scenario does. The scenario runs in live mode: it waits for
blocks, never mines them, and checks rejections against the mempool only. All four scenarios
behave as on M13's regtest runs ([`results/rehearse/`](results/rehearse/)):

| Scenario | What happened | vbytes mined |
|---|---|---|
| unchallenged | funded at h_e = 201 (batchHeight 203, deadline 207). The kickoff is refused at tip 201 (`non-final`) and confirms at 204. The payout is refused early (`non-BIP68-final`) and confirms at 210, h_k + 6, paying the template exactly | 998 |
| late | no kickoff. The sweep of the deadline connector is refused at tip 216 (`non-final`) and confirms at 218. The kickoff is then refused (`missing-inputs`). Refunds are refused one block early and confirm at 241, the block after the escrow expiry | 495 |
| stall | no kickoff; refunds at 272, the block after the expiry | 390 |
| challenged | challenged at h_k + 5; assert_init and commit[0] at h_k + 6; commit[1] beside them is refused (`too-large-cluster`), as on regtest; commits 1–4 at h_k + 7, assert_final at h_k + 8, 3 blocks to spare; payout_asserted at h_A + 6, paying the template exactly | 424,117 |

- **Probes the chain overtook.** One probe in the run went unchecked: the challenged scenario's
  kickoff-before-batchHeight. The chain passed batchHeight between two polls, so the run records
  it as missed instead of failing; the other three scenarios checked it.
- **The unchallenged challenge probe.** The `challenge(after payout)` probe is refused with
  `bad-txns-prevout-null`. That scenario funds no challenger coin and uses a placeholder, so Core
  stops at the placeholder before it reaches KC.
- **Costs at 100 sat/kvB.** The assert chain's presigned fees are about 42,800 sats, against
  846,938 at 2 sat/vB in M9. The reserve, with connectors and anchors, is 45,945 sats, and the
  challenge pays 47,000.
- **What a run funds.** About 62,000 sats for unchallenged, late or stall:
  - 10,000 of escrow;
  - the 5,000-sat bond;
  - the 45,945-sat reserve;
  - the 1,000-sat deadline connector.

  A challenged run adds the challenger's coin, 67,000 sats, so about 130,000 in all. The wallet
  watches the keys, so what a run returns (the bond, the unspent reserve, payouts, refunds)
  funds the next one.

## 4. Testnet4

testnet4 is reached through its own Bitcoin Core node in WSL (`/root/testnet4`, RPC 48342),
synced from genesis in this session. Its wallet `m16` was funded with 59,910 tSats swept from two
local testnet4 wallets, in block 158490. The keys are salted with a secret kept outside the
repository. Results are in [`results/testnet4/`](results/testnet4/).

**Who mines testnet4, and what they take.** [`blocks.txt`](results/testnet4/blocks.txt) lists
every block of the run with its difficulty and the lowest fee rate it includes.

- One address mines most blocks at minimum difficulty, each timestamped 1,201 s after its parent.
  They come about every 20 minutes, near the 2-hour future limit. **This miner includes nothing
  under 1,000 sat/kvB (1 sat/vB).**
- Every 3 to 12 blocks, until block 158546, a real-difficulty block arrived, timestamped 1 to 2
  hours before its parent. Blocks 158501, 158513 and 158530 include transactions from about 100
  sat/kvB. Blocks 158521, 158539 and 158546 include nothing under 1,000. Then none came until
  158570: 24 blocks, about 6 hours of wall time.
- After each real-difficulty block, other addresses mine five or six coinbase-only
  minimum-difficulty blocks within about a minute, until the timestamps are back at the limit.

So a transaction at the relay floor waits for the next real-difficulty block. The graph presigns
at 100 sat/kvB, so every graph transaction did.

**unchallenged** ([`scenario-unchallenged.json`](results/testnet4/scenario-unchallenged.json)):
bond 2,500 sats, burn 1,000, deadline connector 500.

| Transaction | Valid from block | Sent at tip | Mined |
|---|---|---|---|
| funding: escrows, bond, reserve, connector (`cf036adf…`, 384 vB) | 158491 | 158490 | 158491 |
| kickoff (`11a8a717…`, 298 vB); nLockTime = batchHeight 158493 | 158494 | 158493 | 158501 |
| payout_unchallenged (`c3b9e3c2…`, 700 vB); h_k + 6 | 158507 | 158511 | 158513 |

- **Refusals.**
  - The kickoff, sent at tip 158491, is refused as `non-final`.
  - The timeout before T_assert is refused as `non-BIP68-final`.
  - After the payout, the challenge is refused as `bad-txns-prevout-null` (the placeholder coin)
    and the timeout as `missing-inputs`.
- **The payout** pays the template exactly.
- **Size and cost.** The graph's two transactions are 998 vB and paid 100 sats. The run took
  5.5 hours, blocks 158490 to 158518.

**What the unchallenged run showed.**

1. **The kickoff confirmed after its deadline.**
   - The deadline connector could be swept from block 158498.
   - The kickoff was sent at batchHeight and waited in the mempool through seven
     minimum-difficulty blocks. It was mined at 158501, the next real-difficulty block.
   - Nobody swept the connector in blocks 158498–158500, so the batch settled. Anyone watching
     could have swept it and turned the batch into refunds.
   - The deadline gives the operator 4 blocks after batchHeight. On testnet4 a floor-rate
     transaction can wait 12.
   - The kickoff's operator-keyed anchor (M9) can carry it at a higher rate. The driver bumps
     only when the market rate is above the presigned rate, and here the two were equal. At
     1,000 sat/kvB the driver's fee-spike path funds bump coins of 120,000 sats each, more than
     the run had.
2. **The driver read heights at the tip.**
   - The run saw the kickoff confirm at tip 158506, after the burst 158502–158506, and took
     that as the kickoff's height.
   - It therefore sent the payout at 158511 instead of 158507. It also missed the early-payout
     probe: at tip 158510 the payout was already final.
   - Commit 74033383 takes each height from the block that mined the transaction, and the
     regtest rehearsal then checks every probe.
   - In this file, `kickoff_height` (158506) is from before that fix. The `transactions` rows
     carry the mined heights.

**What came back.** The wallet imports every key the run pays. After unchallenged it held 58,845
sats, all spendable: the bond, the unspent reserve, the payouts, the refunds and the anchor. The
run cost 1,065 sats:

- 965 for the funding, where the wallet put 581 sats of change into the fee instead of making a
  change output;
- 100 for the graph.

That left too little to run late at the same amounts.

**late** ([`late.log`](results/testnet4/late.log)): bond 1,500 sats, burn 500, deadline
connector 400, to fit the 58,845 sats left.

| Transaction | Valid from block | Sent at tip | Mined |
|---|---|---|---|
| funding (`bfd10981…`, 844 vB) | 158519 | 158518 | 158521 |
| sweep of the deadline connector (`58718d49…`, 105 vB); nLockTime 158525 | 158526 | 158525 | 158530 |
| refunds ×3 (`6305556d…`, `e4e6b9c6…`, `7f0f549b…`, 130 vB each); nLockTime 158548 | 158549 | 158549 | 158570 |

- **Refusals.**
  - The sweep, sent at tip 158524, is refused as `non-final`.
  - After the sweep, the kickoff is refused as `missing-inputs`: the batch can no longer settle.
- **Missed probes.**
  - The funding confirmed at batchHeight, so the kickoff was already final and went unprobed.
  - Blocks 158546 to 158549 came between two polls, so the early refund went unprobed.
- **The refunds waited 21 blocks.**
  - Buyers sign their refunds when posting, at the market rate, here 100 sat/kvB. They waited
    through 21 minimum-difficulty blocks.
  - After 6 hours the driver stopped with `transactions unconfirmed after 6 h` and wrote no
    JSON. The log and the chain are the record.
  - The refunds pay keys the wallet holds, so a child (`5950c761…`, 226 vB) spent all three. Core
    priced it to bring the package to 6 sat/vB, a 3,663-sat fee.
  - Child and refunds were mined in 158570. That was the next real-difficulty block, whose lowest
    included rate was 100 sat/kvB, so the refunds alone would have been included too.
- **Cost.** 4,713 sats: 1,000 for the funding, 11 for the sweep, 39 for the refunds and 3,663
  for the child. The sweeper kept 389 of the connector's 400 sats and the bond stayed unspent.
  Everything else came back to the wallet.

**challenged** ([`scenario-challenged.json`](results/testnet4/scenario-challenged.json)): at
1,000 sat/kvB (`ZKC_SAT_PER_KVB=1000`), bond 1,500, burn 500, connector 400.

- At 100 sat/kvB, challenged would need real-difficulty blocks for:
  - the kickoff;
  - the challenge;
  - three sequential assert steps.

  Those blocks came 7 to 24 blocks apart, so the operator would miss T_assert. At 1,000 sat/kvB
  any block that includes transactions takes them.
- The run funds about 900,000 sats: the assert chain's 424,117 vB, and the 428,000 the
  challenge pays the operator. After the faucet, the wallet held 1,053,490.
- On a live chain the driver challenges at once (commit ed76d17d): a challenge in the last
  allowed block can lose that block to a miner that includes nothing.

| Transaction | Valid from block | Deadline | Mined |
|---|---|---|---|
| funding | | | 158595 (batchHeight 158597) |
| kickoff (`2367df2e…`, 298 vB) | 158598 | 158601, the last block before anyone may sweep the connector | 158604 |
| challenge (`58e91f00…`, 212 vB) | 158605 | h_k + T_challenge − 1 = 158609 | 158609 |
| assert_init + commit[0] (92,442 vB) | | | 158610 |
| commits 1–4 (330,031 vB) | | | 158611 |
| assert_final (`fc5e9f6a…`, 442 vB) | | h_k + T_assert − 1 = 158615 | 158614, then 158618 after a reorg |
| payout_asserted (`67c540a8…`, 692 vB) | A + T_disprove = 158624 | escrow refunds open in 158625 | 158625 |

- **The payout paid the template exactly.** It also completed the full assert chain on a public
  chain: 424,117 vB of Winternitz commitments for the ceremony's verifying key. The refusals
  were:
  - the kickoff before batchHeight: `non-final`;
  - the unchallenged payout after the challenge: `missing-inputs`;
  - the timeout after the payout: `missing-inputs`.
- **Every timing margin was used up**:
  - Blocks 158597 to 158603 include nothing but their coinbase, from three addresses other than
    the bot. So the kickoff waited 7 blocks and confirmed 3 blocks after its deadline. Nobody
    swept the connector.
  - Blocks 158605 to 158608 were empty too. The challenge, sent at once, confirmed in the last
    block it could.
  - The assert chain took one block for assert_init and commit[0] and one for the commits.
    Then the chain reorganized.
- **A 4-block reorg moved assert_final past T_assert.**
  - assert_final was first mined at 158614, a block before its deadline. A 4-block reorg from
    158611 then put the chain on blocks that did not include it.
  - It was mined again at 158618, two blocks after the timeout opened at h_k + T_assert = 158616.
  - In 158616 and 158617, anyone could have mined the timeout. That would have burned the honest
    operator's bond and paid the slasher.
  - `getchaintips` shows 20 abandoned branches of 1 to 4 blocks with tips from 158582 to
    158625. The minimum-difficulty and
    real-difficulty miners keep replacing each other's blocks.
  - The graph's MARGIN of 2 blocks is its reorg allowance, and this chain exceeded it.
  - The driver read the heights when it saw each confirmation, so the JSON records
    assert_final at 158614 and `blocks_to_spare_before_timeout` 1. The table above gives the
    chain as it stands after the run.
- **The payout and the refunds opened together.** assert_final's re-mining at 158618 put the
  payout's valid block at 158624. The escrows expire at 158624, so their refunds were valid from
  158625, the block that mined the payout. Any one buyer could have refunded instead, and the
  payout, which spends every escrow, would have become invalid.
- **Cost.** The wallet went from 1,053,490 sats to 628,233, so the run cost 425,257 sats, about
  1 sat/vB on what it mined. It took 5.0 hours, blocks 158594 to 158625.

What the challenged run shows: the graph's timeouts of 6 and 12 blocks assume blocks that
include transactions and reorgs no deeper than 2. testnet4 had runs of 7 empty blocks and a
4-block reorg within one settlement. Every deadline still held, but without a block to spare.
On mainnet, empty blocks are rare and a reorg of more than 1 block is rarer still. These T values
were sized for regtest. A deployment would size each T from the chain's own record of empty
blocks and reorg depth.

**Not run on testnet4: stall.** It runs the same CLTV refunds as late.

## Reproduce

```sh
# Phase 2, in WSL as root: three contributors, the beacon named at height H.
NODE_DIR=/root/zkc-tools/node SNARKJS_MODULES=<run.sh build>/deps/node_modules \
  bash research/zk_clearing/m16/ceremony_sim.sh contribute <accounting16x4_committed.r1cs> <phase-1 .ptau> 3 H /srv/zkc-m16-ceremony
# Once block H exists:
bash research/zk_clearing/m16/ceremony_sim.sh finish <r1cs> <phase-1 .ptau> <hash of block H> /srv/zkc-m16-ceremony
# The testnet batch, proved with the ceremony's key; then BitVM's scripts for that key.
bash research/zk_clearing/m16/prove_batch.sh /srv/zkc-m16-ceremony <run.sh build> /root/zkc-m16 --small
ln -s /root/zkc-m9/target /root/zkc-m16/target   # an M9 build (build.sh, 18 public inputs)
/root/zkc-m16/target/release/zkc-m1 prepare /root/zkc-m16
# The scenarios: on a timed regtest chain, or on testnet4 once wallet m16 is funded.
BITCOIN_BIN=/root/opt/bitcoin-31.1/bin bash research/zk_clearing/bitvm/scripts/run-m16.sh /root/zkc-m16 rehearse
BITCOIN_BIN=/root/opt/bitcoin-31.1/bin bash research/zk_clearing/bitvm/scripts/run-m16.sh /root/zkc-m16 address
ZKC_BOND_SATS=2500 ZKC_BURN_SATS=1000 ZKC_DEADLINE_SATS=500 BITCOIN_BIN=/root/opt/bitcoin-31.1/bin \
  bash research/zk_clearing/bitvm/scripts/run-m16.sh /root/zkc-m16 testnet4 unchallenged
ZKC_BOND_SATS=1500 ZKC_BURN_SATS=500 ZKC_DEADLINE_SATS=400 BITCOIN_BIN=/root/opt/bitcoin-31.1/bin \
  bash research/zk_clearing/bitvm/scripts/run-m16.sh /root/zkc-m16 testnet4 late
```
