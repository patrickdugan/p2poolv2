# M12: can a disprove reach a block?

ROADMAP item 4 asked for a disprove path that relays. M12:

- weighs every disprove transaction;
- measures what hashing state costs in BitVM's scripts, which decides whether leaves can be cut
  under the relay limit;
- works out how long the disprove window has to be when only some miners accept a non-standard
  transaction.

Measured 2026-11-01 on the M1 build (BitVM `c620dbf` with patch 0001, 18 public inputs) in WSL2
Ubuntu 24.04: 253 s, 12.4 GiB peak. Rows marked *est.* are arithmetic on measurements.

## 1. Every disprove fits a block, and 430 of 432 do not relay

`zkc-m1 disproves <work>` builds the 432 BitVM leaves and the witness each one takes for the
honest assertion. It adds a control block for A's tree (451 leaves, depth 9) and weighs M2's
disprove shape: KB with its committee signature, then A, with a burn output and a P2TR reward
output. M1's shape (A alone) is 271 WU lighter.

Patch 0001 gained `api_disprove_witnesses_for_all_leaves`, which builds every leaf's witness from
one pass over BitVM's segments. The per-leaf call rebuilds the segments, a full pairing, each
time. The new function is additive, and M1 to M3 behave as before. Patch 0002 still applies on
top of it.

| 90th percentile | 3,661,294 |
| **Heaviest (leaf 425)**: a 3,948,062-byte script and 848 witness items (10,412 bytes) | **3,960,303** |

- **The script is nearly all of it.** Witness and transaction overhead add 2,800 to 12,241 WU
  per leaf, median 4,848.
- **Cross-checks against the transactions M1 and M2 mined.**
  - Leaf 431 weighs 955,994 WU here, and M2's mined disprove through it weighed 955,993.
  - Leaf 30 weighs 2,628,329 WU here in M1's shape. M1's x + r disprove through it weighed
    2,627,539, 790 WU less, because a disproving witness's items can be shorter.
- **The margin is thin but real.** Leaf 425 is 31,697 WU under the default template. A disproving
  witness differs from the honest one only in item lengths. The margin is three times leaf 425's
  entire witness.
  - A chunker or verifying key that grew the largest leaf by 1% would push its disprove out of
    every block a default-configured node builds.
  - The same check belongs in any change to the circuit's public inputs or to BitVM.
- **M3 has the same heaviest disprove,** *est.*: its leaves are M1's leaves 0–1 and 74–431 with
  the same scripts, and its A tree (366 leaves) has the same depth.

## 2. Cutting leaves under the relay limit does not work with this verifier

A leaf relays only below 400,000 WU. Cutting a leaf in two means each piece has to hash the
state that crosses the cut: the earlier piece as its output, the later piece as its input.
BitVM's state hash is BLAKE3 written in Script. Its sizes, from `blake3_compute_script`:

| State hashed | Script bytes |
|---|---|
| 64 bytes (2 field elements) | 77,777 |
| 128 bytes (4) | 154,922 |
| 192 bytes (6: BitVM's normalized Fp12) | 232,067 |
| 384 bytes (12) | 463,502 |
| 448 bytes (14) | 540,647 |

- **The pairing leaves cannot be cut small enough.** Inside a Miller-loop step the Fp12
  accumulator crosses any cut. BitVM keeps it as 6 field elements. A piece between two cuts
  hashes it on both sides: 2 × 232,067 = 464,134 bytes. That is over the 400,000 WU limit before
  any arithmetic.
  - 424 of the 432 disproves are over 1 MWU, so nearly every leaf would need such cuts.
  - Getting under the limit needs a cheaper way to commit to the state than BLAKE3 in Script.
    That is a change to BitVM's design, outside this project.
- **Even where cutting is possible, it multiplies the assert.** Each cut adds a value that the
  operator commits in the assert chain. That costs about 0.9–1.0 kvB per value: M1 commits 463
  values in 422,650 vB, M3 commits 378 in 336,528 vB, and the 85 values between them cost
  86,122 vB.

| Piece budget (script bytes) | Pieces (M1's 432 leaves) | New committed values, at least | Assert chain, *est.* |
|---|---|---|---|
| 350,000 | 3,888 | 3,456 | about 3.5 MvB |
| 200,000 | 6,700 | 6,268 | about 6.1 MvB |

The table ignores the hashing cost above, so it is a lower bound. At 3.5 MvB every challenge would
cost about 8 times M1's assert, and the challenge price and bond with it.

## 3. Reaching a miner that takes non-standard transactions

The disprove therefore goes straight to miners who accept it outside the P2P network:

- P2Poolv2's own node operators, who build their own templates;
- pools that take direct submissions.

M2 and M3 mined their disproves this way on regtest, through `generateblock`, a miner's own
template.

**The window.** Suppose miners with a fraction h of the hashrate accept the disprove. The chance
that none of the next T blocks is theirs is (1 − h)^T. To keep that below ε:

T ≥ ln ε / ln(1 − h)

| Hashrate share accepting it | T for ε = 10⁻³ | T for ε = 10⁻⁶ | Chance within M2's 144-block mainnet window |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5% | 1,379 blocks (9.6 days) | 2,757 (19.1 days) | 51.4% |
| 1% | 688 (4.8 days) | 1,375 (9.5 days) | 76.5% |
| 2% | 342 (2.4 days) | 684 (4.8 days) | 94.6% |
| 5% | 135 (0.9 days) | 270 (1.9 days) | 99.94% |
| 10% | 66 | 132 (0.9 days) | > 99.99% |
| 25% | 25 | 49 | > 99.99% |
| 50% | 10 | 20 | > 99.99% |

What follows for the graph:

- **T_disprove comes from h.** M2's mainnet sketch of 144 blocks is safe only if miners with
  about 5% of the hashrate or more take the disprove. With P2Poolv2's own hashrate alone, h is
  the pool's share.
- **A long T_disprove costs only the challenged path.** It delays payout_asserted. It also
  lengthens T_refund, since T_refund ≥ T_kickoff + T_assert − 1 + T_disprove + r (M2 section 2),
  so buyers' escrow is locked longer. The unchallenged path does not depend on T_disprove.
- **The heaviest disprove fills a block.** At 3,960,303 WU it is 99% of a block. A miner that
  includes it gives up nearly a whole block of other fees, so it has to pay about that: f × 990
  kvB at market rate f. M2's bond sizing uses this figure.

## 4. Reading

- **The route is through miners.** Cutting the leaves under the relay limit is ruled out for the
  pairing chunks by the cost of hashing their state, and would multiply the assert at least
  eightfold elsewhere.
- **The disprove window is set from the hashrate that accepts non-standard transactions**, not
  from watcher latency alone.
- **Every disprove fits a default block template today, with 31,697 WU to spare** on the
  heaviest. Any change to the verifier needs this check again.

## Reproduce

With M1's work dir prepared (M1.md) and the patch 0001 in this branch applied by `build.sh`:

```sh
/root/zkc-m1/target/release/zkc-m1 disproves /root/zkc-m1
```

It writes `m12/disproves-18pub.json` under the work dir. [`results/m12/`](results/m12/) holds this
run's copy and its timings.
