# M17: one statement for everything

Run 2026-11-03. Everything below was executed and measured unless marked *est.* The demo keys and
the Groth16 setup are public and insecure, so **none of it is safe with real funds**; it is regtest
only.

The question (ROADMAP item 2): can M15's packed joined circuit, which proves seller signatures,
buyer escrow terms, fee caps, expiries and ownership of the sold outputs behind one hashed public
input, run through M14's one-input BitVM pipeline on M13's bound, anchored graph, so that a single
regtest run covers signatures, ownership, fees and heights?

- **Machine:** the machine of [M14.md](M14.md) (WSL2 Ubuntu 24.04, 19 GB VM), Bitcoin Core v31.1.0
  on a private regtest node, RPC port 18813, work dir `/root/zkc-m17`.
- **Circuit:** `owned_tree_pk_16x4` = `OwnedSignedTreePacked(16, 4, 4, 2, 3, 5)`
  ([`joinedtree/circuits/`](../joinedtree/circuits/owned_tree_pk_16x4.circom)), set up with the
  insecure deterministic beacon on M6's power-20 phase-1 file.
- **Verifier:** BitVM `c620dbf` with patches 0001 and 0002 (`NUM_PUBS = 1`, `NUM_HASH = 363`),
  unchanged.
- **Graph:** `ZKC_GRAPH=anchored ZKC_BIND_HEIGHT=1` at the presigned 2 sat/vB.

## Headline

**It runs.** All five scenarios behave as designed on one proof of the joined statement: the honest
unchallenged and challenged runs pay exactly the template, the tampered run is disproved at the final
pairing leaf, the late run is swept at the deadline and refunds, and the stall run refunds at the
signed expiries. The kickoff's lock time, the deadline connector and every escrow's CLTV refund come
from numbers inside the proven statement.

## 1. What changed, and why

### 1.1 The escrow leaf binds `escrowExpiry`

Before M17 the escrow leaf was `Poseidon5(active, escrowId, funding, feeCap, refundScriptId)`.
`escrowExpiry` was signed by the buyer and range-checked against `batchHeight`, but the committee,
which sees only `escrowCommitment`, could not tell which expiry the operator proved with. An
operator could prove with one expiry and give the committee a graph with another. The leaf is now

```
escrowLeaf[b] = Poseidon6(activeBuyer, escrowId, funding, feeCap, refundScriptId, escrowExpiry)
```

in [`clearing_tree_signed.circom`](../circuits/clearing_tree_signed.circom), and mirrored in
`signedtree/statement_signed_tree.js` (so the joined statement script too), in `escrow_commitment`
in `template.rs`, and in the cost-split script's label. M4's own circuit
(`circuits/signed_clearing.circom`) has its own Poseidon5 leaf and does not share the template, so
it is unchanged.

**Cost, measured.** Poseidon6 against Poseidon5 is 33 constraints per buyer slot:

| Circuit | Before | After | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| `signed_tree_16x4` (M11) | 112,921 | 113,053 | +132 |
| `owned_tree_pk_16x4` (M15) | 152,805 | 152,937 | +132 |

The verifying keys changed with the circuit (M11 16x4 `d8937b82…1dc9c6c4`, previously
`ef1a22e3…bf4ad364`; joined `25ba85a4…771edfeb`, previously `a5bc11e6…`). The 64x8 and 128x8
columns of SIGNED_TREE.md and the other joined sizes were not rerun; each grows by 33 per buyer slot,
*est.* (+264 at B = 8).

**Regenerated, consistently.** `bitvm/vectors/signed-*` (M14's batch, proof, statement, example
proof, verifying key, the 16x4 statement) were rebuilt by `prove-batch.sh signed_tree_16x4`;
`signed-m11-128x8-statement.json` was recomputed from M11's 128x8 `valid.json` with the new leaf
(no 128x8 proof was rerun; the file only feeds the Rust hash test). New: `owned-*`. The pinned
verifying-key digits in `prove-batch.sh` and the digits quoted in `convert/tests/signed_vectors.rs`'s header changed
with them. M14.md and the files under `results/m14/` and `signedtree/results/` record the earlier
leaf and are left as the record of those runs.

### 1.2 The template (`Kind::OwnedTreePacked`)

`template.rs` gains `Kind::OwnedTreePacked { sales, buyers }`, selected by the circuit name
`owned_tree_pk_<S>x<B>` in `batch/circuit.txt`. Its statement has 4 + 4B + 1 values: M11's, then the
window tree's `windowRoot`. `build_signed` (shared with M14) checks, in order:

1. `statementHash` and every value are below r, and the statement hashes to `statementHash`
   (`PoseidonChunked`: for 21 values `Poseidon2(Poseidon12(v[0..12]), Poseidon9(v[12..21]))`);
2. `saleCommitment` and `escrowCommitment` open to the circuit input, **the escrow leaves now
   including each `escrowExpiry`**, and `buyerId[b]` is the sale list's;
3. **new:** the last value equals `opening["window_root"]` (the root the watchers publish) and the
   circuit input's `windowRoot`;
4. the payout, refund and escrow ids are the ids of the labels the graph pays, and the money
   checks (prices sum to `totalPayout`, outputs conserve the escrowed value, no dust).

The returned `Template` now carries `batch_height` and, per escrow, `expiry` (read from the input
that step 2 opened). M14's kind exposes them too (905,000 and 905,144).

### 1.3 The driver (`zkc-m2`)

`zkc-m2` is built twice from the same source. The default build (18 public signals) still runs
M1's accounting statement; a `ZKC_PUBS=1` build reads `batch/circuit.txt` as `zkc-m1` does, builds
the template with `Kind::build_hashed`, and turns every statement-mutating scenario into a
single-hash one: `tampered` is S' with 10,000 sats of refund moved from buyer slot 1 to slot 0, which
hashes to h' (public signal `statementHash`), the committee presigns for S', and the operator
asserts h' with the proof of S.

In bound runs of a kind that fixes `batchHeight` (`Kind::height_from_template`, the joined kind):

- the **kickoff's nLockTime** is the template's `batchHeight` (230) and the **deadline connector**
  opens at `batchHeight + DEADLINE_AFTER_BATCH` (234);
- each **escrow's CLTV refund** uses that buyer's committed `escrowExpiry` (257 for all four,
  `batchHeight + 27`); the driver asserts every expiry is at least `batchHeight + ESCROW_AFTER_BATCH`
  (22);
- the proof is made before the run, so the driver mines from the chain's start (200) up to
  `batchHeight - BATCH_HEIGHT_AFTER_FUNDING - 1` = 227, funds the escrows in 228, and aborts if the
  chain is already past that. `run-m17.sh` starts every scenario on a fresh chain for that reason;
- the refund step takes the earliest and latest expiry (they are per escrow now) for its early-refund
  probe and its wait.

M14's batch (`batchHeight` 905,000) keeps M13's relative heights; the template's heights only drive
the run where the chain can reach them.

### 1.4 The batch

`prove-batch.sh <work> owned_tree_pk_16x4` runs `joinedtree/run_size.sh pk 16 4` on
`$PTAU` (default `/root/zkc-m6/pot20_final.ptau`) for the setup and its checks, then
[`batch_joined.py`](batch_joined.py) turns the fixture's `valid` batch (sale slot s sells the output
at the s-th lowest position of the packed window tree, signed by its registered owner's order key)
into the regtest batch: refunds 38,000 / 31,000 / 12,000 / 17,000 (as M14, so no output is dust),
`batchHeight` 230, `escrowExpiry` 257, `orderExpiry` 374. `sign_tree.js` signs it, snarkjs proves it
with the same key, `statement_joined_tree.js` recomputes the statement, and the script refuses the
batch unless the proof's public signal equals circomlibjs's `statementHash` and the statement's last
value equals the opening's `window_root`.

```sh
PATH=/root/zkc-tools/node/bin:/root/zkc-tools/bin:$PATH PTAU=/root/zkc-m6/pot20_final.ptau \
  bash research/zk_clearing/bitvm/prove-batch.sh /root/zkc-m17 owned_tree_pk_16x4
```

batch's height is fixed, so the run must be able to mine to it, and rehearse mode's timer-mined chain
would not stop where the statement needs it. `run-m17.sh` is that script with a fresh chain per
scenario. [`results/m17/`](results/m17/) holds every scenario's JSON and log, the proof system's
`run.sh` results for both circuits, the conversion reports, the leaf survey, the timings and the
test summaries.

## 3. What is bound now, and what is not

**One proof, one hash, now covers:**
- each seller's order signature and each buyer's escrow-terms signature (BabyJubjub EdDSA);
- that the signer of every sale is the registered owner of the sold output in the window tree whose
  root is in the statement (membership, position order and distinctness);
- fee budgets within each buyer's signed fee cap, and each buyer's equation
  `funding = cost + fee + refund`;
- `batchHeight + 4 <= orderExpiry` and `batchHeight + 22 <= escrowExpiry`.

**What the committee checks from the opened statement** (so the graph it presigns is one the proof
supports): the payout script ids, the refund script ids, the escrow ids, the money, the window root
(against the root the watchers publish), and **now every escrow's expiry**, from `escrowCommitment`.
The graph's heights are those numbers: kickoff lock time, deadline connector, CLTV refunds.

**Not bound:**
- **That the root is the root of a canonical anchor.** The template compares it with the root in the
  opening; a committee still needs watchers that rebuilt the window tree from the sharechain and the
  key registrations (JOINED_TREE.md). The opening in this run is the fixture's world, not a chain.
- **That a sold output stays unspent after the anchor**, and that the sharechain pays the sale's
  seller. The graph's lock-before-payout depends on the sharechain enforcing a timeout; it does not
  enforce CSV or timelocks (undisclosed F8). Nothing here claims otherwise.
- **That `escrowId` names the funded outpoint.** The ids are field encodings of labels; the harness
  funds a fresh outpoint per escrow. A real graph hashes the outpoint into the signed id and spends
  exactly it.
- **Order keys are not payees' keys.** The BabyJubjub keys behind `sellerId` and `buyerId` are bound
  to sharechain addresses by M10's registrations in the ownership tree, not to the regtest payees,
  whose secp256k1 keys derive from labels.
- **The batch has every slot active.** Padding ran only in the R1CS check
  (`joinedtree/run_size.sh`), as in M14.
- **The committee is one key**, the Winternitz secrets come from a public seed, and the Groth16
  setup is the insecure deterministic one. Disproves other than leaf 364 (the binding leaf) need a
  miner to include a non-standard transaction (M12).
- **The expiries' margins are the circuit's and the driver's, not the template's.** The template
  returns the committed expiries; the driver asserts the 22-block margin and the circuit proves it.
- **Timeouts** were sized on regtest (ROADMAP item 1 still open).

## 4. Checks run

- `signedtree/run_size.sh 16 4` and `joinedtree/run_size.sh pk 16 4` (through `prove-batch.sh`): valid
  proof verifies, the public signal equals circomlibjs's `statementHash`, 10 and 15 invalid inputs
  are refused in the templates the manifest names, the re-split batch proves a different hash, a
  tampered signal, `x + r` and a forged witness are rejected, the padded batch satisfies the R1CS,
  and the statement carries the root.
- `build.sh` with `ZKC_PUBS=1` and with the default 18: both build and `cargo test --release`
  passes: 4 + 4 + 7 conversion tests, 24 template tests in `zkc-m1` (6 new) and 27 in `zkc-m2`
  (the same 24 plus its 3 graph tests).
- Python: `signedtree` 13 tests, `joinedtree` 24, `bitvm` `test_batch_signed` 6 and the new
  `test_batch_joined` 6 (run in separate processes: both import a module named `gen_inputs`).
- The 18-signal path of `zkc-m2` on regtest, as a regression check on M1's batch in `/root/zkc-m1`
  (M2 graph): `unchallenged` pays the template (payout 700 vB) and `tampered` is disproved at the
  final pairing leaf (238,999 vB). The other scenarios on that build were not rerun.

## What M17 does not show

- No live-chain run of this statement (`ZKC_CHAIN=live` with a template-driven height would wait for
  a chain to reach `batchHeight`; the driver mines to it on regtest only, or waits).
- No run above 16 x 4 and no 1,024-slot proof.
- The identity-point case of one public input (hash with a zero first window) was not exercised; this
  batch's hash ends in byte 30.
